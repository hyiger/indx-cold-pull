INDX Cold Pull

Generates a guided cold-pull procedure for the Prusa CORE One INDX, to clear a partially clogged nozzle. Prusa's built-in wizard (M1702) is not enabled for INDX, so there is no in-firmware equivalent.

Do these at the printer first.

None can be set or read over G-code — they are menu-only settings, so nothing here can check them for you. Each fails silently rather than loudly.

Settings

80 °C is the field-verified pull temperature; raise in 5–10 °C steps only if the plug will not release. Flush is capped at 290 because the firmware clamps settable targets there. These are INDX-frame values — its sensor sits 17 mm above the tip and reads high, so do not substitute numbers from MK4 or XL guides.

Before your next print, put these back

  1. Filament Sensor → ON
    Left off, the printer will not detect a runout or jam during normal printing.
  2. Auto Retract → ON
    Left off, filament is not retracted out of the melt zone at the end of a print, which invites the next clog.
  3. Refit the PTFE tube
    Printing without it will not feed reliably.
  4. “Serial Printing Screen” → back ON, if you want it
    Optional. Only affects the serial-print UI, not printing itself.

What it does

Pick toolHeating requires a latched tool, so the pick must come first.
Flush~60 mm of PLA at the flush temperature to displace old material.
PackSmall extrusions while cooling toward 180 °C, so the plug casts the nozzle interior.
Deep coolFan-assisted to 60 °C with a fixed dwell, so the plug grips the debris.
PullReheat to the pull temperature in two stages to avoid overshoot, raise motor current, retract hard.
RestoreUndo every session change: motor current, extrusion guard, stall detection.
Wipe & dockRewarm the nozzle so residue releases instead of dragging cold strings, then dock automatically — no manual parking. The USB-file route also gets the firmware's end-of-print wastebin wipe, warm.

Prompts appear on the printer's screen and wait for a knob press — six in all: confirm setup, insert the PLA (not before), start the purge (from here, watch the nozzle tip for melted PLA flowing out), confirm what came out, start the pull, remove the strand. Success looks like three thin strands with visible dark debris; repeat until the tip comes out clean, then re-enable the sensor and auto retract and refit the PTFE tube.

Verified against Prusa-Firmware-Buddy 6.6.3+15625. Use at your own risk — this drives a hot nozzle and a high-current motor.
