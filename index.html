INDX Cold Pull

Generates a guided cold-pull procedure for the Prusa CORE One INDX, to clear a partially clogged nozzle. Prusa's built-in wizard (M1702) is not enabled for INDX, so there is no in-firmware equivalent.

Do these at the printer first.

None can be set or read over G-code — they are menu-only settings, so nothing here can check them for you. Each fails silently rather than loudly.

  • Filament Sensor → OFF Otherwise the printer grabs and autoloads the filament while you are inserting it by hand.
  • Auto Retract → OFF Otherwise the printer can treat the filament as retracted and discard the extrusion and pull moves — the procedure appears to run while nothing happens.
  • Remove the PTFE tube from this tool The pulled plug travels up and out of the top port. With the tube fitted there is nowhere for it to go.
  • Light-coloured PLA on hand, and stay at the printer PLA shows the extracted debris clearly. The procedure waits for knob presses at five points, and the heaters switch off after ~30 minutes unattended.
  • USB Serial only: “Serial Printing Screen” → OFF, then reboot Settings → Hardware → Experimental Settings. Left on, a 5-second inactivity timeout runs the end-of-print sequence mid-procedure, docking the tool while commands are still being sent. This crashes the printer with E move without tool.

Settings

80 °C is the field-verified pull temperature; raise in 5–10 °C steps only if the plug will not release. Flush is capped at 290 because the firmware clamps settable targets there. These are INDX-frame values — its sensor sits 17 mm above the tip and reads high, so do not substitute numbers from MK4 or XL guides.

What it does

Pick toolHeating requires a latched tool, so the pick must come first.
Flush~60 mm of PLA at the flush temperature to displace old material.
PackSmall extrusions while cooling toward 180 °C, so the plug casts the nozzle interior.
Deep coolFan-assisted to 60 °C with a fixed dwell, so the plug grips the debris.
PullReheat to the pull temperature, raise motor current, retract hard.
RestoreUndo every session change: motor current, extrusion guard, stall detection.

Prompts appear on the printer's screen and wait for a knob press. Success looks like three thin strands with visible dark debris; repeat until the tip comes out clean, then re-enable the sensor and auto retract and refit the PTFE tube.

Verified against Prusa-Firmware-Buddy 6.6.3+15625. Use at your own risk — this drives a hot nozzle and a high-current motor.
